---
title: Build a fair mystery
description: Lock the solution, maintain the clue ledger, and audit solvability.
slug: docs/guides/mystery
---

Before drafting the reveal, record the culprit, motive, means, opportunity, timeline, evidence chain, alibis, secrets, and reveal order. Lock the solution once the author approves it.

Each clue records discovery, interpretation, access, reader visibility, dependencies, payoff, and red-herring purpose. A later clue may clarify earlier evidence, but planting new decisive evidence retroactively requires an approved retcon.

Publication remains blocked when the solution is unsupported, alibis contradict the timeline, important clues lack explanation, or the reader never received enough evidence to solve the case.
