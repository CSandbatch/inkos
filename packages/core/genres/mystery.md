## Fair-play contract

The reader receives every decisive fact before the solution. Criminals and mistaken witnesses may misdirect; narration may not invent false facts or conceal an indispensable observation. The final reconstruction must link identity, method, opportunity, motive, chronology, access, and concealment to disclosed evidence.

## Contemporary evidence

Digital artifacts are not self-authenticating. Separate content from provenance, normalize timestamps, distinguish account access from physical identity, document camera limitations, and corroborate biometric or forensic interpretations. Models and detectors may suggest leads but never establish identity, intent, guilt, or factual truth.

## Revision priority

Repair the causal model, evidence ledger, access matrix, and reader projection before revising style. Never repair a mystery by hiding information the reader needs to detect the defect.
