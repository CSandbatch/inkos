## Genre Prohibitions

- Advance the plot through illogical coincidences
- Make supporting characters stupid in order to serve the protagonist
- Deliver a climax without setup

## Narrative Guidance

Adjust the narrative focus to the specific genre.
Keep the chain of cause and effect complete.
Character behavior should be driven by motivation, not by what the plot needs.
