# ADR-0009 · Genre-neutral platform, mystery as reference pack

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-04

## Context

Only fair-play mystery has enforcement machinery — 22 rules, deterministic validators, an
adversarial solver, a reader projection. The repository also carries a web-fiction corpus
targeting a near-opposite audience.

## Decision

Position as a **genre-neutral governed production platform**, with fair-play mystery as the
**reference pack** that proves the pack architecture can express and enforce real genre
constraints.

Mystery is the proof, not the identity.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Mystery-only.** Sharpest pitch, smallest market.
- **Web-fiction first.** Larger market, worse first market: governance reads as friction to
  someone shipping five chapters a week.

## Consequences

- **The caveat must be stated, not buried.** On day one a literary-fiction author gets
  provenance, obligations, closure, and attribution — and no genre validators. Q4.
- A thin generic craft pack (chronology, scene obligation, POV consistency, promise/payoff)
  is the cheap fix; several are already fair-play dependencies.
- Mystery is the beachhead: its constraints are decidable, which is what keeps the governance
  layer honest.
