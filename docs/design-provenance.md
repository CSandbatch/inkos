# Fair-play 2026 design provenance

The executable `fair-play-detective-2026` and `english-prose-patterns-2026` packs derive from a user-supplied design specification that consolidated historical fair-play principles, retired discriminatory conventions, added contemporary digital and forensic evidence rules, and catalogued English prose constructions for editorial inspection.

The source attachment is not committed. InkOS publishes independently structured schemas, validators, tests, and rewritten documentation under the repository license. Prose-pattern findings are editorial observations, not AI-authorship detection.
