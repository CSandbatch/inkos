# Code of conduct

NovelGraph contributors and community members must communicate with respect, critique work rather than people, protect private drafts and identities, and avoid harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact shown on the GitHub repository. Reports will be handled confidentially and evaluated in context.
